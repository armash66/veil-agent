Agency Overview & Historical Background

The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) was formed on 15 August 1969, superseding the previous INCOSPAR (Indian National Committee for Space Research). Its headquarters is established at Antariksh Bhavan, New BEL Road, Bengaluru, Karnataka. ISRO operates under the administrative authority of the Department of Space, which is directly overseen by the Prime Minister of India.

Major National Flagship Missions

Chandrayaan-3 (Lunar South Pole)

Soft-landed near lunar south pole on 23 August 2023, making India the fourth nation to land on the Moon and first in the southern polar region.

Aditya-L1 (Solar Observation)

India's first solar observatory mission inserted into a halo orbit around Sun-Earth Lagrange Point 1 (L1) on 6 January 2024.

Gaganyaan (Human Spaceflight)

Indigenous crewed orbital spacecraft designed to carry three astronauts to Low Earth Orbit (LEO) with launch escape verification.
